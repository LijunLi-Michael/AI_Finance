1 Lending — borrowing without a bank

Pool deposits, borrow over-collateralized, interest set by code from utilization, liquidation enforces solvency. No loan officer, no credit check.

3 When incentives break — the hangover

The same trick that grew DeFi — paying for growth with tokens — set up the 2022 crash: Terra/Luna → Three Arrows Capital → FTX. For the post-mortems, see the Terra/Luna and FTX readings in the companion pack “AI Finance — Optional Further Reading.”

Three things to take away

**1. Emissions = rented liquidity** — pay for growth with a token, and the liquidity leaves when the token does.

**2. Real yield vs printed yield** — is the return real cash flow (fees), or an inflating token?

**3. Reflexivity + self-collateral = blow-ups** — backing a system with its own token works until confidence breaks.
