# **Part 2 · How do I run my own? (optional)**

Pick the one that matches your computer:

- [Install OpenClaw on a Mac mini](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpmv3XPm0hw) Ty Chen · 6 min

- [Install OpenClaw in a Windows VM / on Linux](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vRQCE5wGF-c) AI Factory · 13 min

- [Install Ubuntu in a VM on an Apple Silicon Mac](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hRpUGzjpiCc) Ksk Royal · 13 min

**Heads-up:** these were recorded in Feb–Apr 2026 and OpenClaw updates every week. If a step fails, follow the official guide: [docs.openclaw.ai/install](https://docs.openclaw.ai/install).

**Why a VM or a spare machine?** An agent can run commands on the computer it lives on. Keeping it separate limits what can go wrong.
